# SmolVLA status

Status: NOT_RUN. No official SmolVLA weights were downloaded or loaded, no official vision/language/state processor was invoked, and no frozen or fine-tuned checkpoint was evaluated. The local ACT and Diffusion models are state-only baselines and must not be described as VLA. Official SmolVLA evaluation depends on a compatible Linux LIBERO environment, pinned model and dataset revisions, action/control protocol mapping and adequate accelerator resources. The CPU FetchPush results are unrelated to SmolVLA performance.
