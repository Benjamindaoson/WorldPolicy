# Compact model-based actor-critic baseline

Status: **CPU_VERIFIED** exploratory simulation. `worldpolicy.fetch_push_mbrl` collected 24 fresh FetchPush episodes (1,043 real transitions, seeds 94000–94023) with alternating privileged teacher and random actions, storing actual reward, next state, success termination and time-limit truncation. It loaded the separately trained 3-member robot dynamics ensemble, trained a reward/termination network for 400 updates, supervised a 28D→4D actor for 400 behavior-cloning warmup updates, and then trained actor and critic for 500 updates with target networks and soft updates. Three-step imagined rollouts were filtered by the 75th-percentile real-replay ensemble variance; 32,000 imagined samples entered critic batches and 9,858 candidate branches were rejected. Time-limit truncation does not mask TD bootstrap; success termination does.

Actual checkpoint `artifacts/v13/fetch_push_mbrl/checkpoint.pt` includes actor, critic, reward/termination model, targets, BC warmup actor, optimizers and normalization. SHA256 and all counters are in `summary.json`; source commit `ce5d8c2`. On independent seeds 95000–95009, BC warmup scored **3/10**, model-based actor-critic **2/10**. This is a negative single-seed result, not evidence of improvement. The reward/termination model lacks a dedicated held-out accuracy audit, the ensemble was trained on a separate earlier dataset, and no FetchPush IQL/model-free matched-budget control was run. Model-based actor-critic is implemented in the literal sense of replay, learned dynamics/reward/termination, imagination, actor/critic and target updates, but **not validated as a superior method**.
